Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Channel Uploader is a private tool run on the owner's own computer. It is used only by the owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (Send or Fall, RootCause TH).

What data it uses

  • It uses Google OAuth to get permission to manage the owner's YouTube channels (YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube).
  • It reads the channel's id and name to confirm the right channel, uploads video files with their title, description and tags, sets the publish time, and can delete a video the owner uploaded with it.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The tool does not collect data about viewers or any other Google user.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
